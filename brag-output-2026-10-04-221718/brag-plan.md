# Brag Plan: NestGH

## What is this app?
NestGH is a Ghana property marketplace for finding rooms and business spaces, with search filters and direct owner contact.

## The angle
Show a real NestGH search becoming a commercial-space search, then make the product's restraint the payoff: useful filters, no invented listings. The feature is backend-ready, but the backend is not configured in this environment, so the film must not imply that a live query returned zero results.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on the actual NestGH hero and its headline, “Find Your Next Place.” Let the familiar green brand and search form establish the product before the category reveal.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The actual “Shops & Spaces” category leads into the implemented “Shops & Spaces to Let” section.
- Show the real location, space-type, rent, size, road-visibility, parking, electricity, and water controls. A search may select an existing option, but must not imply a real listing exists.
- Reveal the implemented “No shops or spaces available yet” empty-state design as a static product state. No listing card, price, property image, owner, availability, or search result is fabricated.

## Outro / punchline
Return to the NestGH wordmark and its real line, “FIND · LIST · RENT.” Close on “Shops & Spaces to Let” and “Business spaces to let.”

## User flow worth showing
Enter NestGH at the real home page → choose the actual Shops & Spaces category → combine commercial search filters → reach the designed empty state without demo properties. The empty-state screen is presented as an implemented design, not as evidence that a live backend query succeeded.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, confident product film for a Ghana property marketplace
- Interpretation: restrained motion, generous holds, real product copy and UI, and a few soft interaction accents.

## Format: landscape — 1280x720
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f5f8f6`
- Accent: `#0f7a4b` (public-site green; `#69c996` in dark theme)
- Text: `#10201a`
- Display font: Bricolage Grotesque
- Body font: Bricolage Grotesque with system sans-serif fallback
- Strongest visual element: NestGH's real green navigation/search UI and the rounded commercial filter panel.

## Share copy (draft)
NestGH brings rooms and business spaces to one place—with useful search filters and no demo listings.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, restrained polish under real UI moments
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: start at 0, sit quietly below the interface, and fade gently at the end.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at `composition/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; estimated 109.96 BPM. Search and empty-state glow pulses follow measured RMS/bass energy near 8.74, 9.83, and 13.11s; the outro follows 17.47, 18.56, and 19.66s. These are optional visual cues, not text timing.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle green glow pulses use the bundled preset's measured RMS/bass energy; no equalizer or waveform.
- SFX posture: sparse and soft; two quiet UI clicks, `click_003.ogg` and `click_002.ogg`.
- Audio-coupled moments: category selection, filter selection, and final wordmark reveal.
- Restraint rule: no loud hits, no voiceover, and no sounds that imply a listing was found.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Find your next place — 3s
Show the real home-page hero and navigation in NestGH's desktop layout. “Find Your Next Place” is the only prominent headline; retain the actual “Rooms and hostels across Ghana, with clear details and direct owner contact” line where it remains legible.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: warm music enters softly.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: restrained, already under the opening.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — One more category — 3s
Move down the real “Popular Categories” row and land on the actual “Shops & Spaces / Business spaces to let” tile.
Sequential/interaction: the tile is highlighted once, as a navigation choice; do not add property tiles.
Audio intent: one subtle click.
Audio-coupled idea: category selection.
Music: steady and understated.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Search the space — 5s
Show the actual “Shops & Spaces to Let” panel and its existing location, type, rent, size, road-visibility, parking, electricity, and water controls. A cursor may select “Office,” an actual filter option, without showing a result.
Sequential/interaction: controls resolve in a readable sequence, then the real Search spaces button is highlighted.
Audio intent: light interface ticks, no success sound.
Audio-coupled idea: selection of the existing type filter and search action.
Music: warm bed continues.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — No invented listings — 5s
Show the actual designed empty-state panel and the exact line “No shops or spaces available yet.” Make clear through framing that this is an empty-state design, not a live database result. No listing cards, images, numbers, owner details, or sample records appear.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: music opens up slightly; no error or success cue.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: steady, restrained.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — NestGH — 4s
Return to the NestGH wordmark with its actual “FIND · LIST · RENT” lockup and the real category line “Business spaces to let.”
Sequential/interaction: logo and category line appear together and hold.
Audio intent: quiet closing accent, then a short music fade.
Audio-coupled idea: final wordmark reveal.
Music: fade to silence by the end.
Transition mood: soft fade to brand background.

**Music mood for this video:** clean and quietly upbeat.
**Audio summary:** A warm low-level music bed supports two restrained product interactions, then gives the NestGH close room to land.

3. Visual hook: green public-site navigation and rounded search/filter controls.
4. UI to show: the homepage hero/category row and the real commercial search and empty-state UI.
5. Shortest satisfying video: 20 seconds, enough for the real filter flow and a clear no-demo-data ending.
6. Tone: polished; a quiet, confident product film.
7. Audio: warm low-volume instrumental with sparse UI taps; no voiceover.
8. Share caption: NestGH brings rooms and business spaces to one place—with useful search filters and no demo listings.
