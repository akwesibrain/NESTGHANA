# NestGH launch video: plan

**Angle:** The real NestGH site, step by step: SEE, SEARCH, COMPARE, CONTACT, MOVE. Every screen is a screenshot of the running site (localhost:3000), filled with presentation-only listings that are served through request interception and never written to the database.
**Length:** 36.2 s at 30 fps. Formats: 1920×1080 (`brag.mp4`) and 1080×1920 (`brag-vertical.mp4`, from mobile screenshots).
**Music:** happy-beats-business-moves vol. 10 (~110 BPM), -5.5 dB (about -20 LUFS), fading out over the end card. No voiceover. Scene cuts sit on the beat grid.
