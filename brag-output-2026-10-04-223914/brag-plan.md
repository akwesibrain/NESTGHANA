# Brag Plan: NestGH

## What is this app?
NestGH is a Ghanaian property marketplace for finding rooms, hostels, and commercial spaces, with location/type search and direct owner contact.

## The angle
Show the product as a real mobile-first journey: search Greater Accra, compare two clearly labeled illustrative spaces, open one detail view, and reach the actual contact-owner action. The only sample record details appear inside this video composition, with persistent labels that say they are illustrative and not live listings. Nothing is added to the NestGH app or backend.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The NestGH mobile header and actual homepage headline, “Find Your Next Place,” fill the vertical frame. A quiet Ghana-specific search cue follows: Greater Accra.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Search and filter the actual commercial-space flow using the existing location and space-type controls.
- Compare two video-only sample cards: a shop in Osu, Accra, and an office in Tema. Both are marked “ILLUSTRATIVE · NOT LIVE LISTINGS”; their made-up rent and size values are visible only as demo content in the video.
- Open the sample shop's details and finish on the real “Contact owner” action, without inventing a phone number, owner identity, or working contact endpoint.

## Outro / punchline
Show the official NestGH logo and the real “FIND · LIST · RENT” lockup. Close on “Search. Compare. Contact.”

## User flow worth showing
Search Greater Accra → review combined search/filter controls → compare clearly marked sample cards → view a sample detail page → tap “Contact owner.” Sample property fields are video-only and never enter production sources or the database.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium mobile-first property product film with subtle Ghanaian grounding
- Interpretation: tall, confident UI close-ups, clean type, measured transitions, green/gold NestGH accents, and no stock imagery.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f5f8f6`
- Accent: `#0f7a4b` (public-site green; `#69c996` in dark theme)
- Text: `#10201a`
- Display font: Bricolage Grotesque
- Body font: Bricolage Grotesque with system sans-serif fallback
- Strongest visual element: NestGH's real mobile navigation, commercial filter panel, rounded cards, green actions, and official logo.

## Share copy (draft)
Search, compare, view, contact. NestGH brings Ghana's room and commercial-space journey into one place. Listing details shown here are illustrative demo content, not live listings.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, modern polish beneath the UI.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: low-volume bed from 0, with a gentle fade at the close.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset in `composition/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, approximately 109.96 BPM. Use nearby strong cues around 4.39, 8.74, 13.11, and 17.47 seconds for restrained interactions and the logo hold, only where readability allows.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle green light pulses tied to measured RMS/bass energy from the cue preset; no waveform or visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse, soft, and matched to search, details, and contact actions.
- Audio-coupled moments: search selection, result comparison, detail-to-contact action, final brand reveal.
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, no loud hits, and no sounds that imply a real listing or contact was verified.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Start the search — 3s
Show the real NestGH mobile header and exact “Find Your Next Place” homepage headline. Add a small search prompt and a subtle location cue for Greater Accra.
Sequential/interaction: location chip appears once.
Audio intent: warm music opens quietly.
Audio-coupled idea: location cue lands on a soft beat.
Music: low instrumental bed.
Transition mood: soft upward slide → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Search and filter — 3s
Show the real Shops & Spaces filter structure as a mobile interface: Location, Space type, rent range, size, road visibility, parking, electricity, water, Clear Filters, and Search spaces. Use Greater Accra as the sample search input and keep “Any type” selected so both sample categories can follow.
Sequential/interaction: a cursor taps the search action. A clear “ILLUSTRATIVE DEMO” tag establishes that the following records are video-only.
Audio intent: a single quiet UI click.
Audio-coupled idea: search action around a measured cue.
Music: steady, restrained.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Compare spaces — 5.5s
Show two real-data-shaped cards with neutral NestGH icon placeholders, not invented property photos:
- SAMPLE LISTING · NOT LIVE — Shop, Osu, Accra — GH₵ 4,500 / month — 120 m².
- SAMPLE LISTING · NOT LIVE — Office, Tema — GH₵ 3,200 / month — 85 m².
Both are explicitly illustrative video demo values, not current NestGH inventory. No owner/contact details are shown.
Sequential/interaction: the first card appears, then the second; keep both fully visible long enough to compare.
Audio intent: two soft card/selection accents at most.
Audio-coupled idea: second card lands on a beat, then both hold.
Music: warm and even.
Transition mood: soft push-in to the first card → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — View and contact — 5s
Show the first sample space in the existing shared detail-sheet visual language: Shop, Osu, Accra, GH₵ 4,500 / month, 120 m², with the persistent “ILLUSTRATIVE · NOT A LIVE LISTING” label. No fake photo, advance, utility claims, owner name, phone number, or WhatsApp number. Highlight the actual “Contact owner” button as the final action; do not pretend it opens a real contact.
Sequential/interaction: card opens into details, then a tap lands on Contact owner.
Audio intent: one restrained tap; no success tone or fabricated call connection.
Audio-coupled idea: CTA tap aligned near a cue.
Music: gentle support.
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Find · List · Rent — 3.5s
End on the official NestGH logo and “FIND · LIST · RENT,” followed by “Search. Compare. Contact.”
Sequential/interaction: logo and words resolve together, then hold.
Audio intent: quiet closing accent and soft fade.
Audio-coupled idea: final brand reveal.
Music: fade out by 20s.
Transition mood: fade to the NestGH palette.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, polished, and lightly upbeat.
**Audio summary:** A soft instrumental bed supports three quiet UI taps and measured beat-aware light pulses, then fades under the NestGH logo.

2. Strongest claim: “Find Your Next Place,” plus clear details and direct owner contact from the site.
3. Visual hook: NestGH's green mobile UI, official blue/gold logo, and Ghana-specific locations.
4. UI to show: actual filter flow, sample comparison cards styled like NestGH, shared property details, and Contact owner.
5. Shortest satisfying video: 20 seconds for all five steps.
6. Tone: polished, premium, mobile-first.
7. Audio: low warm instrumental, quiet clicks, no voiceover.
8. Share caption: NestGH brings Ghana's room and commercial-space journey into one place; all property data shown in this video is illustrative.
9. Flow: search → filter → compare → view details → contact owner.
